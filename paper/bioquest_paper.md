# BioQuest: Gamified Learning of Mediterranean Marine Biodiversity

*(Draft paper — see https://github.com/yespi/bioquest for the platform)*

## 1. Introduction
BioQuest gamifies learning Mediterranean marine species identification using AI-powered quizzes and satellite exploration.

## 2. Platform Features
See `docs/features.md` for complete feature documentation.

## 3. Educational Design
- Spaced repetition (Ebbinghaus)
- Test-enhanced learning (Roediger & Karpicke)
- AI-generated distractors from YOLOFauna embedding space
- EUMETSAT satellite imagery for environmental context
